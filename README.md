# Runway and Burn Calculator

Your runway is the cash on hand divided by your net monthly burn, where net burn is monthly expenses minus monthly revenue. This tool computes the number of months that cash lasts and the month it runs out, and tells you plainly when revenue already covers expenses so there is no runway limit.

**Live demo:** https://0xelitesystem.github.io/runway-and-burn-calculator/

## What it does

Enter cash on hand, monthly expenses, and any monthly revenue. The tool shows months of runway, the net monthly burn, and the approximate month the cash reaches zero. When revenue meets or beats expenses, it reports that the cash is not being drawn down rather than returning a misleading number.

Runway assumes burn and revenue hold steady, which they rarely do, so treat the figure as a planning baseline. Closing the gap with revenue extends every month of it.

## Aesthetic

A tear-off desk calendar: a red header band, a perforated page edge, and a large month count, with the zero-cash date read off like a deadline.

## Not financial advice

This tool performs arithmetic on the numbers you enter. It is not financial, tax, or accounting advice, and it does not account for your specific circumstances. Verify any figure before acting on it.

## Privacy

Everything runs in your browser. Nothing you type is sent anywhere, stored, or saved. Closing the tab clears it.

## Use it

Open `index.html` in any modern browser, or host it as a static page. No build step, no dependencies, no network calls.
